ABOUT AISLING

Beauty looks good on you!

I have trained extensively in the beauty industry and hold a Diploma in Beauty Therapy, as well as being a certified Nail Artist. I have been a fully qualified Beauty Therapist and Nail Technician for over 12 years, with experience across both salon and freelance environments.

I specialise in Gelish manicures, Yumi Lash Lifts, brows, threading and spray tans, offering quality treatments with detailed results at very competitive prices.
